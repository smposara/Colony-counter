# Tester invitation – closed testing

Messages to send to closed-test testers.

**Easiest:** send one link, **https://cc.amphur.in.th/join.html**. The page (from
`app/web/join.html`, published with the web version) walks testers through joining
the Google Group, becoming a tester and installing, in Thai or English, and
explains the "App not available" message. Use it as [OPT-IN LINK] below and delete
the group step.

**iPhone and iPad users** can't join the Play test. Send them
**https://cc.amphur.in.th/ios.html** (`app/web/ios.html`): it shows, with a video, how
to add the web app to the Home Screen and how to send feedback. join.html links to it.
Web testers do not count towards Google Play's 12 testers.

Otherwise replace the parts in [brackets]:

- **[OPT-IN LINK]**: Play Console → Test and release → Testing → Closed testing →
  Manage track → Testers → "How testers join your test" → Copy link. The link
  only works after Google has approved the first closed-test release.
- **[GROUP LINK]**: only if you use a Google Group for testers. Otherwise delete
  step 1, and add each tester's Gmail address to the email list in the Play
  Console before you send.

The Thai texts have no ครับ/ค่ะ, so add the one you use.

## Email – English

**Subject:** Invitation to test Colony Counter (Android) before release

Hello [name],

You're invited to test **Colony Counter** before it is released on Google Play.
The app photographs agar plates and counts the colonies automatically. It also
calculates CFU/mL or CFU/g from the dilution. It works offline, in Thai and
English, and no data leaves your phone.

Google requires a new app to have **at least 12 testers who stay in the test for
14 days in a row** before it can be published, so your help really matters.

**You need**
- an Android phone;
- a Google (Gmail) account [the one you gave me].

**How to join (about 2 minutes)**
1. [If using a Google Group] Join the tester group: [GROUP LINK]
2. On your phone, open this link: [OPT-IN LINK]
3. Tap **Become a tester**.
4. Tap **Download it on Google Play** and install the app.

If Play Store says the app is not found, check that Play Store is using the same
Google account you joined with. Tap your profile picture at the top right to
switch accounts.

**Please**
- stay in the test and keep the app installed for at least **14 days**, and don't
  tap "Leave the program";
- try the app a few times, on real plates or on plate photos from the internet;
- tell me what is confusing, what it counts wrongly, or what is missing:
  sarapukdee@gmail.com, or just reply to this message.

Thank you very much for helping!

Pongsak Sarapukdee

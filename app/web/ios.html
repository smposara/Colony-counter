Good to know

Keep your data safe. Your photos and counts are stored in the web app on this device only. Safari can delete a website's stored data after a few weeks without use, unless the site was added to the Home Screen, so always open it from the icon, and export a backup from the app's menu now and then.

  • Updates arrive by themselves: close the app completely and open it again to get the newest version (the version is shown under About).
  • Internet is needed to open the web app. Counting then runs on your device: your photos and results are not uploaded.
  • Other browsers such as Chrome on iOS 16.4 or later can also add it to the Home Screen from their Share menu, but Safari is the simplest.
  • Camera. The web app uses the iPhone's own camera, so the in-app level, focus and glare guide of the Android app is not shown. Hold the phone level over the plate and avoid reflections.
  • Have an Android phone too? You can also join the Android test: become a tester.
